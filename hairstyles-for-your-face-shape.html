Which hairstyle suits your face shape?

Your face shape is the single biggest clue to a haircut that genuinely flatters you. Below are the most flattering hairstyles for the five face shapes — oval, round, square, heart and long — for women and men. Want the shortcut? How to find your face shape

Pull your hair back and look straight into a mirror or a front-camera photo. Compare four things: the width of your forehead, your cheekbones, your jaw, and how long your face is from hairline to chin.

  • Oval — slightly longer than it is wide, with a softly rounded jaw. The most versatile shape.
  • Round — about as wide as it is long, with full cheeks and a soft jaw.
  • Square — a strong, angular jaw with forehead, cheeks and jaw a similar width.
  • Heart — widest at the forehead, tapering to a narrower, often pointed chin.
  • Long (oblong) — noticeably longer than it is wide, with a straighter jaw.

Best hairstyles for an oval face

Oval faces have balanced proportions, so almost anything works — the goal is simply to keep that balance and avoid hiding it.

For women

  • Curtain bangs & long layers
  • Blunt bob or a sleek lob
  • Balayage and money-piece colour

For men

  • Textured crop or quiff
  • Pompadour
  • Classic crew cut

Go easy on: heavy, face-covering styles that hide your balanced proportions.

Best hairstyles for a round face

Round faces are about as wide as they are long, so the aim is to add height and length and create a few angles.

For women

  • Long layers below the chin
  • Side-swept styles & curtain bangs
  • Volume at the crown

For men

  • Pompadour or quiff
  • Faux hawk
  • High fade with height on top

Go easy on: blunt bobs at the cheeks and very short, rounded cuts that add width.

Best hairstyles for a square face

Square faces have a strong, angular jaw. Soft texture and movement balance those sharp lines beautifully.

For women

  • Soft layers and waves
  • Side-swept fringe
  • Longer, wispy lobs

For men

  • Textured crop
  • Soft quiff, medium length
  • A clean buzz or crew also looks sharp

Go easy on: heavy blunt bobs at the jaw and very severe straight lines.

Best hairstyles for a heart face

Heart faces are widest at the forehead and taper to the chin, so it helps to add a little width and softness around the jaw.

For women

  • Chin-length bobs and lobs
  • Curtain bangs & side parts
  • Layers with volume near the jaw

For men

  • Mid-length flow
  • Textured fringe
  • Medium length with movement

Go easy on: heavy volume at the crown and tight slicked-back styles that widen the forehead.

Best hairstyles for a long face

Long (oblong) faces are longer than they are wide. The goal is to add width at the sides and avoid extra length on top.

For women

  • Curtain bangs or a soft fringe
  • Waves and blunt, chin-grazing bobs
  • Width and volume at the sides

For men

  • Medium length with side volume
  • Textured fringe / crop
  • Keep height on top modest

Go easy on: long, flat styles and tall volume on top that lengthen the face further.

Face shape & hairstyle questions

How do I find out my face shape? +
Pull your hair back and look straight into a mirror or front-camera photo, then compare your forehead width, cheekbones, jaw and face length. Oval is slightly longer than wide with a soft jaw; round is about as wide as it is long; square has a strong angular jaw; heart is widest at the forehead and tapers to the chin; long is noticeably longer than wide. Trimly's AI can also measure it from a selfie in seconds.
What is the most versatile face shape? +
Oval is generally considered the most versatile, because its balanced proportions suit almost any cut, length or fringe. Most styling "rules" are really about recreating that balance for other face shapes.
Do these tips work for men too? +
Yes — the same principles apply: add height for round faces, soften strong jaws on square faces, balance the forehead on heart faces, and keep height modest on long faces. Trimly recommends gender-appropriate styles for both women and men.
