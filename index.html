FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

How does AI Style Match work? +
You take a photo of your face. Trimly analyses your face shape, recommends flattering hairstyles, and generates a realistic try-on of any look on your own face — so you can see it before you ever sit in the chair.
Is Trimly free? +
Yes. Trimly is free to download and use on iOS and Android. Style discovery, salon browsing, saved looks and My Cuts are all included.
Where is Trimly available? +
Trimly is launching across India with a curated catalogue of over 1,300 local salons including ratings, hours and contact details. More cities are being added all the time.
What happens to my photo? +
Your face photo is processed only to analyse your face shape and render your try-on, then deleted — it is never used for anything else. AI Style Match is available to users 18 and over, with explicit consent, in line with India's DPDP Act 2023.
Can I book a salon in the app? +
Trimly helps you discover and contact salons — see their hours, reviews and price range, and call them in one tap. In-app booking is on our roadmap for a future release.
