Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trimly ("Trimly", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Trimly mobile app and this website. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your verified email address and basic profile details to create and secure your account.
  • Face photos (AI Style Match) — if you choose to use AI Style Match, you provide a photo of your face. This is biometric-adjacent personal data and is handled with special care (see §3).
  • Your content — saved styles and salons, My Cuts entries and notes, and reviews you write.
  • Usage & device data — limited technical information needed to operate the service reliably and securely.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide style recommendations, AI try-on images, salon discovery, and your saved history.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and content secure across devices.
  • To moderate reviews and maintain a safe, trustworthy community.
  • To understand and improve how the app performs. We do not sell your personal data.

3. AI Style Match & your face photo

AI Style Match is available only to users aged 18 and over and requires your explicit consent before any photo is processed.

  • Your face photo is used only to analyse your face shape and to generate your try-on images.
  • The raw photo is processed ephemerally and deleted after analysis. It is not retained for training or any other purpose.
  • Generating a try-on may transfer your photo to a third-party image-generation provider that may process it outside India. This happens only with your explicit cross-border consent and under a zero-data-retention agreement.
  • You can decline AI Style Match and still use the rest of the app.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us run Trimly (such as hosting, authentication, and AI processing), each bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. Raw face photos are deleted promptly after processing. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data; reviews you posted are anonymised rather than removed.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may access, correct, or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent (including for AI Style Match) at any time. You can delete your account from within the app, or contact us using the details below.

7. Children

Trimly is intended for users aged 14 and above. AI Style Match is restricted to users 18 and over. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children below these ages without verifiable parental consent.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at privacy@trimly.info.
